Identify the research design of this study.

DESIGN TYPES:
1. RCT: Randomized Controlled Trial - participants RANDOMLY assigned to treatment/control groups
2. QUASI-EXPERIMENTAL: Non-random group assignment (intact classes, convenience sampling) but still has treatment and control groups
3. PRE-POST: Single group measured before and after intervention (no separate control group)
4. CROSSOVER: Groups alternate between treatment and control conditions
5. OTHER: Other designs not fitting the above categories

CLASSIFICATION GUIDELINES:
- Look for explicit mentions of "random assignment" or "randomized"
- "Intact classes" or "existing sections" usually indicates quasi-experimental
- If only one group is used with pre/post tests, it's pre-post
- Pay attention to the Methods section for design details
