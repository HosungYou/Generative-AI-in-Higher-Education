Based on the paper content, classify the learning outcome according to Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001).

TAXONOMY LEVELS (from lowest to highest):
1. REMEMBER: Retrieving relevant knowledge from long-term memory (recognize, recall, list, define)
2. UNDERSTAND: Constructing meaning from instructional messages (interpret, exemplify, classify, summarize, explain)
3. APPLY: Carrying out or using a procedure (execute, implement, solve, demonstrate)
4. ANALYZE: Breaking material into parts and detecting relationships (differentiate, organize, attribute, compare)
5. EVALUATE: Making judgments based on criteria (check, critique, judge, assess)
6. CREATE: Putting elements together to form a novel whole (generate, plan, produce, design)

If the outcome spans multiple levels, use "mixed".

CLASSIFICATION GUIDELINES:
- Focus on what the ASSESSMENT actually measures, not what instruction aimed for
- Multiple-choice tests typically measure REMEMBER or UNDERSTAND
- Problem-solving tasks typically measure APPLY or ANALYZE
- Project-based assessments typically measure CREATE or EVALUATE
- Self-report surveys about learning strategies may measure different levels depending on content
