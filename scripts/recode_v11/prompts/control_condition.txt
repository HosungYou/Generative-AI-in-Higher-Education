Identify what the control or comparison group received in this study.

CONDITIONS:
1. NO_TREATMENT: Control group received no intervention at all
2. TRADITIONAL: Control group received traditional/conventional instruction without AI
3. HUMAN_TUTOR: Control group received human tutoring or mentoring
4. ALTERNATIVE_TECH: Control group used different technology (non-AI)
5. ACTIVE_CONTROL: Control group received an active but different treatment
6. WAITLIST: Control group was on a waiting list to receive treatment later
7. NOT_REPORTED: The paper does not clearly describe the control condition

GUIDELINES:
- "Business as usual" or "regular instruction" → TRADITIONAL
- "No feedback" or "no assistance" → NO_TREATMENT
- Look in the Methods/Procedure section for control condition descriptions
