Identify the education level of the study participants.

LEVELS:
1. K-12: Elementary, middle school, or high school students
2. UNDERGRADUATE: University/college undergraduate students
3. GRADUATE: Master's or doctoral students
4. PROFESSIONAL: Professional training, medical residents, in-service teachers
5. MIXED: Participants from multiple education levels

GUIDELINES:
- Look for participant descriptions in the Methods section
- "University students" or "college students" → UNDERGRADUATE
- "Medical students" → context-dependent (could be undergraduate in some countries)
- "Pre-service teachers" in education programs → usually UNDERGRADUATE or GRADUATE
- "In-service teachers" → PROFESSIONAL
