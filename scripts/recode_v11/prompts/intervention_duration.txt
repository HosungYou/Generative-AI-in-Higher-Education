Determine how long the GenAI intervention lasted.

DURATIONS:
1. SINGLE_SESSION: One-time use or single class session (under a few hours)
2. <1_WEEK: Multiple sessions but less than one week
3. 1-4_WEEKS: One to four weeks of intervention
4. 1-3_MONTHS: One to three months (approximately one semester quarter)
5. >3_MONTHS: More than three months (full semester or longer)
6. NOT_REPORTED: Duration not clearly stated

GUIDELINES:
- Look for specific timeframes in the Methods/Procedure section
- "One semester" in US context ≈ 15-16 weeks → >3_MONTHS
- "One semester" in some Asian contexts ≈ 8-10 weeks → 1-3_MONTHS
- "4-week module" → 1-4_WEEKS
- A single lab session → SINGLE_SESSION
