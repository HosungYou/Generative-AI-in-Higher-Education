Identify the academic discipline or subject area of the study.

DISCIPLINES:
1. STEM: General science, technology, engineering, mathematics
2. HUMANITIES: History, philosophy, arts, literature
3. SOCIAL_SCIENCES: Psychology, sociology, political science
4. MEDICINE: Medicine, nursing, health sciences, pharmacy
5. BUSINESS: Business administration, management, economics, marketing
6. EDUCATION: Education, pedagogy, teacher training
7. LANGUAGE: Language learning, ESL/EFL, writing, linguistics
8. CS: Computer science, programming, information technology
9. ENGINEERING: All engineering disciplines
10. MIXED: Study spans multiple disciplines

GUIDELINES:
- CS and Engineering are listed separately from STEM for granularity
- "Writing course" without language learning focus → may be LANGUAGE or HUMANITIES depending on context
- "Programming course" → CS
- If the course is about AI/ML → CS
