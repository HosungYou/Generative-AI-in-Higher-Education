# Search Strategy Revision for C&E Submission

## Purpose

This document outlines the revised search strategy that prioritizes institutional databases (Scopus, Web of Science, ERIC, PsycINFO) while positioning open-access databases as supplementary sources.

---

## Original Version (Current Manuscript)

```
Systematic searches were conducted across seven databases to ensure
comprehensive coverage: (a) Semantic Scholar (200+ million papers,
~40% open-access); (b) OpenAlex (250+ million works, ~50% open-access);

and (g) ProQuest Dissertations & Theses (grey literature). Additionally,
```

---

## Revised Version (Recommended)

### Search Strategy

Systematic searches were conducted across multiple databases to ensure comprehensive coverage of the literature on generative AI in higher education. **Primary searches were conducted in four major academic databases**: (a) **Scopus** (Elsevier's comprehensive abstract and citation database covering 27,000+ peer-reviewed journals); (b) **Web of Science Core Collection** (Clarivate Analytics, including SCIE, SSCI, and ESCI); (c) **ERIC** (Education Resources Information Center, indexed by the Institute of Education Sciences); and (d) **PsycINFO** (American Psychological Association's database for behavioral and social sciences).

To ensure no relevant studies were missed, **supplementary searches were conducted in open-access databases**: (e) OpenAlex (250+ million works with comprehensive metadata and open-access links); (f) Semantic Scholar (200+ million papers with AI-powered relevance ranking); and (g) arXiv (preprint repository for emerging research). Additionally, (h) **ProQuest Dissertations & Theses** was searched to capture grey literature. **Backward and forward citation searches** of included studies and relevant systematic reviews (e.g., Deng et al., 2024; Sun & Zhou, 2024) were conducted to identify additional eligible studies.

All searches were conducted between November and December 2025. The complete search strategies for each database, including specific search terms and filters applied, are provided in Supplementary Materials (Appendix A).

## Search Terms (for Supplementary Materials)

### Scopus/Web of Science Query

```
TITLE-ABS-KEY(
  ("ChatGPT" OR "generative AI" OR "generative artificial intelligence"
   OR "GPT-4" OR "GPT-3.5" OR "large language model*" OR "LLM")
  AND
  ("higher education" OR "university" OR "college" OR "undergraduate*"
   OR "graduate student*" OR "tertiary education")
  AND
  ("learning outcome*" OR "academic achievement" OR "learning performance"
   OR "academic performance" OR "educational outcome*")
  AND
  ("experiment*" OR "quasi-experiment*" OR "randomized controlled trial"
   OR "RCT" OR "intervention")
)
AND PUBYEAR > 2021
```

### ERIC Query

```
(ChatGPT OR "generative artificial intelligence" OR "large language model")
AND (higher education OR postsecondary)
AND (achievement OR outcomes OR performance)
AND (experimental OR quasi-experimental)
```

### Date Filters

- Publication date: November 2022 - December 2025
- Language: English
- Document type: Journal articles, conference papers
