## Overlap Handling Decision

### Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Keep all (Recommended)** | Independent analysis, enables comparison | Minor overlap criticism possible |
| **Remove overlapping** | Completely independent pool | Reduces N by ~5 studies |
| **Report both versions** | Transparent, comprehensive | More complex presentation |

### Decision: Keep All + Sensitivity Analysis

**Rationale**:
1. Overlap is minimal (~11%)
2. Our analysis uses different methodology (3-level model)
3. We test a unique theoretical framework (Cognitive Dependency)
4. Sensitivity analysis will show robustness

### Recommended Sensitivity Analysis Statement

> As a sensitivity analysis, we re-ran the main analyses excluding 5 studies that overlapped with prior meta-analyses (Banihashem et al., 2024; Li, 2023; Mahapatra, 2024; Song & Song, 2023; Yilmaz & Yilmaz, 2023). Results remained consistent (g = [value], 95% CI [lower, upper]), indicating that our findings are not driven by these overlapping studies.

## Differentiation Statement for Introduction

> Unlike prior meta-analyses that included studies published through 2024 (Deng et al., 2024; Sun & Zhou, 2024; Liu et al., 2025), the present study incorporates **31 studies published in 2025**, representing the most current experimental evidence on generative AI in higher education. This temporal extension is particularly important given the rapid evolution of GenAI tools and pedagogical integration strategies.
