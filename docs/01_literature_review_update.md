# Literature Review Update: Related Meta-Analyses

## Overview

This document summarizes meta-analyses on Generative AI (GenAI) / ChatGPT in higher education published between 2024-2025.

## Related Meta-Analyses

### 1. Sun & Zhou (2024)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Title** | Does Generative Artificial Intelligence Improve the Academic Achievement of College Students? A Meta-Analysis |
| **Journal** | Journal of Educational Computing Research |
| **DOI** | [10.1177/07356331241277937](https://doi.org/10.1177/07356331241277937) |
| **Studies (k)** | 28 papers (65 independent studies) |
| **Participants** | 1,909 |
| **Target** | College students only |
| **Effect Size** | Hedges' g = 0.533 (95% CI [0.408, 0.659]) |
| **Publication** | August 27, 2024 |

**Key Moderators**:
- Activity type, sample size, generated content type showed within-group differences
- Most effective when: GenAI generates text, collaborative learning, sample size 21-40

---

### 2. Liu, Zuo & Lu (2025)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Title** | The Impact of ChatGPT on Students' Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis |
| **Journal** | Journal of Computer Assisted Learning |
| **DOI** | [10.1111/jcal.70096](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcal.70096) |
| **Studies (k)** | 37 |
| **Target** | All educational levels |
| **Effect Size** | Hedges' g = 0.577 (95% CI [0.395, 0.759]) |
| **Publication** | July 2025 |

**Key Findings**:
- Greater effects in social sciences
- 5-10 weeks intervention duration most effective
- Declarative knowledge > procedural knowledge
- Traditional classroom + ChatGPT > flipped learning

---

### 3. Ma & Zhong (2025)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Title** | A Meta-Analysis of the Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Learning Outcomes |
| **Journal** | Journal of Computer Assisted Learning |
| **DOI** | [10.1111/jcal.70117](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcal.70117) |
| **Studies (k)** | 34 |
| **Target** | All levels |
| **Effect Size** | g = 0.68 (p < 0.001) |
| **Publication** | September 2025 |

**Analysis Dimensions**:
- Cognitive, competency, and affective dimensions
- Moderators: discipline, duration, knowledge type, prior knowledge, tool type

### 4. Deng et al. (2024) - TARGET JOURNAL

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Title** | Does ChatGPT enhance student learning? A systematic review and meta-analysis of experimental studies |
| **Journal** | **Computers & Education** (Target Journal!) |
| **DOI** | [10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105224](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105224) |
| **Studies (k)** | 69 papers (62 meta-analyzed) |
| **Target** | Predominantly university level |
| **DVs** | Academic performance, affective-motivational states, higher-order thinking, self-efficacy, mental effort |
| **Publication** | December 2024 |

**Key Results**:
- Academic achievement, affective-motivational states, higher-order thinking: improved
- Mental effort: reduced
- Self-efficacy: no significant effect

### 5. Liu X. et al. (2025)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Title** | Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence on K-12 and Higher Education Students' Learning Outcomes |
| **Journal** | Journal of Educational Computing Research |
| **DOI** | [10.1177/07356331251329185](https://doi.org/10.1177/07356331251329185) |
| **Studies (k)** | 49 |
| **Target** | K-12 and Higher Education |
| **Effect Sizes** | Learning achievement: g = 0.857, Learning motivation: g = 0.803 |
| **Publication** | April 2025 |

**Key Findings**:
- Greater impact on higher education students
- Text-based interaction > mixed media
- Motivation effects decrease over time (novelty effect)
