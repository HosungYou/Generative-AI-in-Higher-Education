### Inclusion Criteria

1. Empirical studies with experimental or quasi-experimental design
2. Higher education context (undergraduate or graduate)
3. Generative AI intervention
4. Quantitative learning outcomes reported
5. Published 2022-2025

### Exclusion Criteria

1. K-12 education
2. No control/comparison group
3. Qualitative only studies
4. Non-English publications
5. Conference abstracts without full data
